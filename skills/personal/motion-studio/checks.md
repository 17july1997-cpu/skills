# checks.md: the studio's taste, one correction at a time

Read before building any board or scene. Check every contact sheet against it before a render. Every time the user
corrects a video, add the fix here, in the user's words where possible, as one line:

`- DO / DON'T: the rule (where it was learned)`

Newest at the bottom of each section. When a rule has held across three videos, propose promoting it to the studio's
`CLAUDE.md` (a studio-wide change: ask first). Never delete a rule silently; strike it through with the reason.

## Content (the user's four hated failure modes, picked at setup)
- DON'T: paste the script's sentence onto the board. The voice says the sentence; the board shows the idea, in a
  title plus about six words. (slide-deck syndrome, setup calibration)
- DON'T: show a number, logo, face, quote or result that does not come from the source material or the user. A
  missing value is `[NEED]`, a missing asset is a dashed pending card. (invented proof, setup calibration)
- DON'T: open a board with everything at t=0 and let it sit while the voice talks. Structure first, then each
  element on its word, nothing still for more than 5 s. (dead board, setup calibration)
- DON'T: ship the default Clean minimal theme or any look that could pass for a stock template; never screen-record
  an export. (template look, setup calibration)
- DO: one claim per board, written as its title.

## Canvas
- DO: every readable word 28 px or more at 1080p (36 px on 9:16). If it does not fit, cut words; never shrink.
  Eyebrow, kicker and credit labels are the only exceptions.
- DO: keep the camera zone (x >= 1241, y >= 586 at 1080p) empty on talking-head boards.
- DO: keep 60 px clear on every side.
- DO: with captions on, keep x60-1180, y930-1020 free of board content. (route-E build test: a note collided with
  the captions)
- DO: give every `.sticker` an explicit height and fill; it stretches to its box otherwise. (route-E build test)
- DO: footage at its true aspect, never cropped to fill a box.

## Motion
- DO: every element lands on the word that names it, never before it, at most ~2 s after.
- DON'T: zoom or slide the whole board like a camera; move the elements.
- DON'T: move anything after it lands (no pulses, bobs, loops), except a stamp on its money word.
- DO: one hero effect per board, not on everything.

## Colour
- DON'T: dark text on a dark fill. On dark panels text is white or a light tint.
- DO: one accent colour per idea.
